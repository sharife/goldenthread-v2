**2026 marks Golden Thread’s 30th anniversary**, and we are celebrating three decades of bringing our communities together to reclaim our narratives as the first American theater company dedicated to the Middle East. **[Will you join us in securing the next 30 years for Golden Thread?](https://goldenthread.my.salesforce-sites.com/donate/?dfId=a0n3Z00000tn4RsQAI)**

> “Golden Thread’s existence has become all the more necessary
> over its 30-year lifespan.” —*48 Hills*

**Golden Thread has remained an unwavering, multicultural voice for Middle Eastern and North African communities** as we continue to witness the devastation of violence and oppression locally and abroad. Our stories defy stereotypes and challenge false narratives with rich humanity. In 30 years, we have centered:

* **CREATIVITY, by producing more than 150 plays, most of them world premieres.** While helping expand the Middle Eastern canon of American theatre, we have been an artistic home to such important artists as Yussef El Guindi, Mona Mansour, and Evren Odcikin.  
* **COMMUNITY, by serving more than 80,000 audience members and employing over 2,000 artists,** majority of whom are women, queer, BIPOC, or of Middle Eastern heritage.
* **COURAGE, by partnering with more than 150 organizations fighting for justice,** and curating work that speaks to the needs of our communities, including our Women’s Day Celebrations, *24 Hours for Palestine*, and MENA Theater Makers Alliance Convenings.

**And our communities need us now more than ever.** As government funding dwindles and censorship rises, our next step is clear: build a strong foundation for growth and stability, ensuring that this essential cultural home continues to thrive.
